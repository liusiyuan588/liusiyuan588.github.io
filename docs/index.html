Biography

Siyuan Liu is a Ph.D. researcher in Chemical Engineering at West Virginia University. The research focuses on the mechanical reliability of solid oxide electrochemical cells, with particular interest in coupled thermal, chemical and mechanical processes.

Current work uses finite element modeling and phase-field fracture methods to investigate stress development, crack initiation and damage in electrodes and electrolytes. Earlier research involved computational modeling of multiphase flow in porous media.

Education

  • M.S. in Safety Science and Engineering, China University of Mining and Technology (2019–2022)
  • B.S. in Safety Engineering, Taiyuan University of Technology (2015–2019)

Research Interests

  • Computational solid mechanics and finite element analysis

  • Phase-field fracture and crack propagation
  • Multiphysics modeling and numerical simulation
  • Scientific computing and engineering applications of machine learning
