About Me

My research focuses on the mechanical reliability of electrochemical energy materials, particularly solid oxide cells under thermal and redox cycling. I use numerical modeling to investigate stress evolution, crack initiation, and interfacial damage.

I am interested in the connection between material behavior, computational methods, and component-scale performance. My work spans finite element analysis, phase-field fracture, coupled-field modeling, and scientific computing.

Research background

  • Solid oxide cell mechanics, degradation, and structural reliability
  • Computational solid mechanics and numerical fracture modeling
  • Earlier work in multiphase flow modeling
  • Scientific computing and applied machine learning
