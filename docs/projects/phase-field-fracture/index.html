From damage to crack growth

PROJECT THEMES / METHODS

Phase-field fractureStaggered solutionNewton iterationsAdaptive load stepping

THE CONTEXT

A regularized phase-field variable represents the transition from intact material to cracked material. Combining this damage field with mechanical equilibrium allows complex crack paths to emerge without explicit crack tracking.

THE QUESTION

How can a crack path be resolved while retaining a stable and interpretable nonlinear solution process?

THE APPROACH

  • Discretize displacement and phase-field variables and assemble the coupled weak-form equations.
  • Use incremental loading with alternate minimization or staggered mechanics–damage updates.
  • Examine energy evolution, convergence behavior, load-step cutbacks, and mesh sensitivity.
