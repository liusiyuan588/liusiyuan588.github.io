Redox-induced stress and fracture in solid oxide cells

Examining how electrode contraction during reduction and expansion during oxidation affect stress transfer and potential cracking in the adjacent electrolyte.

The figure is a schematic illustration, not a computed result. Add actual simulation output when ready.

Research question

Under what mechanical conditions can redox-induced deformation lead to electrolyte fracture?

Modeling approach

Combine finite element stress analysis with a phase-field description of fracture to examine loading sequence, layer mismatch, and mechanical constraints.
