Thermal stress in layered cells

PROJECT THEMES / METHODS

Finite elementsPlane stress / plane strainThermoelasticityParametric analysis

THE CONTEXT

Layered electrochemical cells experience different thermal strains across electrodes and electrolytes. Modeling the resulting mechanical constraints helps identify stress concentrations and potential failure locations.

THE QUESTION

How do thermal expansion mismatch, layer thickness, and interfaces shape the stress state in a multilayer solid oxide cell?

THE APPROACH

  • Represent the cell as a layered continuum with material-dependent elastic and thermal properties.
  • Apply thermal loading and appropriate mechanical constraints; solve the coupled displacement equilibrium problem.
  • Compare stress distributions in the electrolyte, electrode support, and regions near interfaces.
