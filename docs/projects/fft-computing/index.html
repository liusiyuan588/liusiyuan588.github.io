THE CONTEXT

Spectral discretizations offer an alternative route to field solutions on regular grids. They are especially useful when exploring heterogeneous material responses and the impact of iterative solver choices.

THE QUESTION

What can frequency-domain operators reveal about efficiency and field resolution in heterogeneous mechanics problems?

THE APPROACH

  • Represent heterogeneous material properties on a regular numerical grid.
  • Evaluate field operators in Fourier space and iterate until the governing residual is controlled.
  • Compare computed strain or stress fields and convergence histories against suitable reference cases.
