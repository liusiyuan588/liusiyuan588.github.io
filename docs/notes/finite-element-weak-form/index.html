From governing equations to the FEM weak form

A short bridge from mechanical equilibrium to element assembly.

Strong form

For a static, small-strain solid, mechanical equilibrium and a linear elastic constitutive relation may be written as:

−∇ · σ = b, σ = C : (ε(u) − ε*)

Weak form

Multiply the balance equation by an admissible test function v, integrate over the domain Ω, and integrate the stress term by parts:

∫Ω ε(v) : σ dΩ = ∫Ω v · b dΩ + ∫Γt v · t̄ dΓ

Discretization

Approximate displacement by nodal shape functions uʰ = Σᵢ Nᵢ uᵢ. The finite element residual follows from the weak form; its derivative yields the tangent stiffness matrix used by Newton iterations.

R(u) = f_int(u) − f_ext, Kₜ = ∂R/∂u
