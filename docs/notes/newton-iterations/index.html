Newton iteration for nonlinear solid mechanics

Residuals, tangents, and step convergence in a nonlinear mechanics solve.

Equilibrium residual

The nonlinear finite element system is expressed by a residual vector R(u), assembled from internal and external forces.

R(u) = f_int(u) − f_ext = 0

Linearized update

At iteration k, linearize the residual around uₖ, solve for Δu, then update the displacement field.

Kₜ(uₖ) Δu = −R(uₖ), uₖ₊₁ = uₖ + Δu

Checks

Track a normalized force residual, the displacement increment, and whether constitutive updates are consistent. A line search or smaller load increment may be necessary when severe nonlinearity causes divergence.

‖R(uₖ)‖ / max(‖f_ext‖, 1) < tolerance
