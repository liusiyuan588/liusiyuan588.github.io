Phase-field fracture: energy and crack regularization

Why a diffuse damage field can represent an evolving crack.

Damage variable

Introduce a scalar field d(x) with d = 0 for intact material and d = 1 for fully damaged material. The sharp crack becomes a diffused zone controlled by an internal length scale ℓ.

0 ≤ d ≤ 1

Illustrative AT2 functional

A common regularized variational form splits the degraded elastic contribution and the crack surface-density term:

E(u,d) = ∫Ω g(d) ψ⁺ₑ(ε(u)) + ψ⁻ₑ(ε(u)) dΩ + Gc ∫Ω [d²/(2ℓ) + ℓ|∇d|²/2] dΩ

Numerical solution

A staggered scheme solves equilibrium for fixed d, then updates the damage field for fixed u. Irreversibility requires damage not to decrease between load increments. These are general modeling concepts, not results from a particular paper.

dₙ₊₁ ≥ dₙ
